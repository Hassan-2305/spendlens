## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No login, no credit card, no paywall. The tool is free because Credex earns only if it connects you to discounted credits you actually want — and only after you see the value.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
A: All prices are sourced directly from vendor pricing pages and verified at submission time. Every number in the audit traces to an official URL, cited in our pricing documentation. Pricing changes — we note the verification date.

**Q: Do I need to enter my email?**
A: No. You get the full on-screen audit with zero information required. Email is optional — only if you want a PDF copy of the report sent to you.

**Q: What's Credex?**
A: Credex sources discounted AI infrastructure credits — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise — from companies that overforecast or pivoted. If the audit finds significant savings, Credex may be able to help you capture even more through discounted credits.

**Q: What if my stack is already optimized?**
A: We'll tell you that. No manufactured savings. If your plan choices are right-sized for your team and use case, the audit says so — and still gives you a shareable report you can use with your finance team.
